# Code of conduct

Lucent follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and considerate, critique code and ideas rather
than people, and accept that a maintainer may decline a change. Harassment,
insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at
faustino.kialungila@gmail.com. Reports are kept confidential. The
maintainer may remove comments, lock threads, or ban a participant,
temporarily or for good, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines
describe.

error LUCENT1018: Borrowed value escapes

A borrowed value cannot be used after suspension.

   ┌─ negative-borrow-after-await.lucent.ts:12:10
12 │   return buffer.length;
   │          ^^^^^^

The borrow originates at a parameter or borrowed call result earlier in this scope. Suspension begins at a preceding `await`; this use would read the borrow after that point. Process the borrow before `await`, or copy needed data into owned memory.
